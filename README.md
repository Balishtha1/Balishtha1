## About

I am a **Master of Computer Applications (MCA) student at the National Institute of Technology, Raipur**, focused on software engineering, artificial intelligence, full-stack development, data analytics, and problem solving.

My experience spans **AI training, software development, real-time systems, machine learning, data analysis, and backend engineering**. At Scale AI (Outlier AI), I worked as an **SDE – AI Training Freelance**, evaluating and improving AI-generated code, debugging model-generated implementations, validating correctness and edge cases, and providing structured feedback to improve LLM reasoning and code-generation quality.

I enjoy building systems where **software engineering and intelligent technologies intersect** — from real-time browser applications to data-driven analytical solutions.

My engineering approach is product-oriented: understand the problem, design around real constraints, build clean interfaces between components, validate correctness, and optimize the parts that actually matter.

### Core Engineering Interests

- **Software Engineering** — algorithms, debugging, APIs, architecture, testing and engineering best practices
- **AI / ML** — supervised learning, regression, classification, predictive modelling and AI evaluation
- **Full Stack Development** — frontend applications, backend services, databases and real-time communication
- **Data & Analytics** — Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Power BI, Tableau and statistical analysis
- **Real-Time Systems** — WebSockets, Socket.IO, WebRTC and low-latency application workflows
- **Product Engineering** — translating technical capabilities into practical software products

### Open To

`Software Engineering` · `AI/ML Engineering` · `Full Stack Development` · `Data Engineering & Analytics` · `Open Source` · `Research & Engineering Collaboration`
